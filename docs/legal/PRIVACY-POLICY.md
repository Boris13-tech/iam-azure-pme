## 2. Our two roles

- **Processor for our customers.** When an organization uses LUXIA to manage the identities and accesses of its people, that organization decides why and how their data is used (the controller). LUXIA processes it on its behalf, under the Data Processing Agreement (`DATA-PROCESSING-AGREEMENT.md`). Requests from those people go first to their organization.
- **Controller for our own needs:** the accounts of the people who sign up an organization, the security of the service, billing **[TO BE COMPLETED when billing exists]**, and our own communications.

## 3. What we process

Only what the service needs. The list below comes from the database schema in use.

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Organization | Name, identifier (slug), verified domains, status | The organization's administrators |
| Identity | Display name, sign-in identifier, life-cycle state | The administrators, or the person's identity provider |
| Microsoft accounts (when the organization uses Microsoft Entra ID) | Microsoft object and tenant identifiers, name, e-mail or user name, as sent by Microsoft at sign-in | Microsoft Entra ID, on behalf of the organization |
| Passkeys and security keys | Public key, credential identifier, type, state, last use. **No biometric data:** a passkey's biometric check stays on the person's device | The person's device |
| Recovery codes | **Hashes only**; the codes are shown once and never stored in clear | Generated by LUXIA |
| Sessions | Session identifier (stored as a hash), times, scope; a SHA-256 hash of the IP address and of the browser's user agent | The person's browser |
| Sign-in records | Method, outcome, assurance, time, reason code | Generated by LUXIA |
| Administration journal | Who did what, when, with what result, including the actions of the LUXIA operator | Generated by LUXIA |
| Accesses and governance | Rights granted, resources, reviews, separation-of-duties rules | The administrators |
| Abuse protection | A hash of the IP address, per time window, kept 2 days | The person's network |

**Note on IP hashes.** The hashes are not salted, so an IPv4 address can be recovered from them by trial. They are therefore personal data, and are treated as such here. See « Gaps to close ».

## 4. Why, and on what legal basis

To be confirmed by counsel for each jurisdiction.

| Purpose | Basis (proposal) |
|---|---|
| Provide the service to the customer organization (sign-in, accesses, administration) | The customer's instructions (processor). For the customer: its own legal basis |
| Secure the service (abuse protection, sign-in records, journal) | Legitimate interest in security; for customers, also their instructions |
| Recovery of access and emergency procedures | Performance of the contract with the customer |
| Legal obligations (for example, notifying a breach) | Legal obligation |

**We do not sell personal data**, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. **[TO BE CONFIRMED by counsel and kept true.]**

## 5. Who receives it

Our subprocessors (`SUBPROCESSORS.md`), only to run the service.

The LUXIA operator never receives a right in a customer organization. Emergency interventions follow a closed, journaled procedure that the organization's administrators can see.

## 6. Where it is stored

See `DATA-RESIDENCY.md`. **Today, Production is hosted in the United States**, and the decided target is the European Union (Frankfurt) before a commercial beta. While data is in the US, a transfer mechanism is needed: **[TO BE COMPLETED by counsel]**.

## 7. How long

See `DATA-RETENTION-AND-DELETION.md`. In short:
- sessions expire after 24 hours, but their records are kept;
- abuse counters are kept 2 days;
- the journal and the sign-in records are append-only. **Today they are kept without a time limit: no automatic deletion exists yet.** The proposed policy, pending legal review and the retention mechanisms, is 1 year by default, extendable up to 3 years when an organization documents a justification ([retention](DATA-RETENTION-AND-DELETION.md)). This notice will state the period only once it is enforced.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on the applicable law: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and complaint to a supervisory authority.

How to exercise them is in `DATA-SUBJECT-REQUESTS.md`. If your organization uses LUXIA, contact it first; we help it answer.
