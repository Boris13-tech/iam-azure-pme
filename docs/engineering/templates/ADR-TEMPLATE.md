## Context
What is true today, with evidence labels: **VERIFIED** (file or observation), **ASSUMPTION**, **UNKNOWN**.

## Decision drivers
The constraints and qualities that decide: security, sovereignty, cost, reversibility, operability.

## Decision
The option chosen, stated so that a test or a review can check it.

## Consequences
- What becomes easier, what becomes harder.
- New trust boundaries or changed ones (update the registry).
- Residual risk, and who accepted it.

## Rollback or exit
How this decision is undone, and until when it can be undone without loss.

## Proof
The tests, rehearsals and gate checks that will show the decision holds.
